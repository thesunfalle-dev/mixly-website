Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Mixly is intended for adults only. The app is an informational catalog and recommendation reference for hookah/shisha flavor mixes. It does not sell, ship, facilitate the sale of, or promote the purchase or consumption of tobacco, nicotine, alcohol, or controlled products.

1. Who operates Mixly

Mixly is operated by Individual Entrepreneur ULADZISLAU YARMAKOVICH, Batumi, Georgia ("Mixly", "we", "us", or "our").

Contact email for privacy, support, and legal requests: support@get-mixly.app.

Postal address: Georgia, Batumi City, Vakhtang Gorgasali st., N116-118, Floor 11, Apartment N58.

2. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Mixly handles information when you use the Mixly mobile application and related hosted legal, catalog, analytics, subscription, support, sharing, translation, and community services. The features described below may vary by app version and platform.

3. Data stored on your device and in your account

Mixly stores some app data locally on your device to keep settings and saved content available between sessions.

  • onboarding preferences, such as selected brands, categories, flavors, and preferred strength;
  • favorite mix identifiers;
  • local app state needed to show your personalized mix feed, daily free-access limits, swipe hints, and paywall state;
  • Labs data, such as custom tobaccos, generated or manual lab mixes, ingredient percentages, and random generation counters;
  • a locally generated Mixly user ID and your saved display name or nickname, used for support and community features;
  • a locally generated Amplitude device ID used for analytics if analytics is enabled;
  • temporary share-card images generated on your device when you choose to share a mix image, where that feature is enabled;
  • language-related app behavior derived from your device locale or in-app language choice.

This information is stored on your device using local storage such as AsyncStorage and temporary app files. If you copy an identifier from your profile, the app puts it on your device clipboard.

On iOS, Mixly 1.0.7 requires you to sign in with Apple or Google to use the main app. Earlier iOS versions allowed guest use. Google sign-in is available in supported Android builds.

Mixly uses Supabase Auth to create and authenticate your account. When you sign in, we process the provider's account identifier, the email address it shares with us, and authentication data. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, the address may be an Apple relay address. A name shared by Apple or Google may prefill your display name during setup. The name or nickname you save becomes part of your profile.

For a signed-in account, Mixly stores a private copy of account data in Supabase to sync and restore it on your devices. This can include saved recipes and ingredients, favorites, onboarding preferences, viewed mix IDs and free-access state, your Mixly user ID, and your display name or nickname. The copy is tied to your Supabase account ID and is accessible through that account.

The Mixly website stores your selected display language in localStorage (mixly-lang). With your consent, Mixly uses Amplitude for optional website analytics and stores a pseudonymous browser identifier and analytics session data in localStorage. Analytics events may include their time, basic browser and device information, the page path without query parameters or fragments, the page type, the display language, the referring site's origin, and actions such as opening the blog or an article, changing the language, and following links to App Store or Google Play. We do not enable automatic interaction capture, heatmaps, session recording, advertising, or social-media tracking.

To change your choice at any time, open Cookies & Analytics from the footer and select the link to open analytics settings. Withdrawing consent stops further analytics collection and clears the stored analytics data from your browser. Amplitude receives website analytics events in the United States and may process network metadata, such as the request IP address, to receive them. Mixly disables IP-based location enrichment for website analytics.

4. Information processed by backend and service providers

Mixly uses Supabase as a service provider to host catalog data, user profile records, support routing, community mix suggestions, moderation data, community reactions, community comments/reviews, published community mix content, RevenueCat webhook routing, and smart share-link pages. Catalog data may include brands, flavors, mix compositions, source references, and related metadata. The app requests this data from Supabase so it can display, rank, and update mixes.

When your device connects to Supabase or to hosting services used for legal pages or app infrastructure, those providers may process technical information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service. This may include IP address, request metadata, device or network metadata, timestamps, error logs, and security logs.

We use this technical information only for service functionality, security, debugging, abuse prevention, and legal or operational compliance. We do not use it for advertising tracking.

5. User identity, support, and community features

Mixly generates a pseudonymous local user ID, such as an ID beginning with "MX-". Mixly uses that ID and your saved display name or nickname, when available, to identify support requests, limit abuse, and attribute community contributions.

The app lets you send suggestions, catalog requests, feedback, and community mix suggestions from inside Mixly. Depending on the feature you use, Mixly may send your message text, message type, app language, source label, generated user ID, saved display name or nickname, mix name, mix description, flavors, tobaccos, strength, timestamps, and related technical request data to Supabase Edge Functions.

In-app support and feedback messages may be forwarded to the Mixly operator through Telegram. They can include your Supabase user ID, your sign-in status when you sent the message, and your Mixly user ID and display name or nickname when available. Community mix suggestions may be stored in Supabase for moderation, review, catalog improvement, and possible publication in the app. If a suggestion is approved, the submitted display name or nickname may appear with the published mix.

Community features may let you react to published community mixes, submit ratings, write review/comment text, edit or delete your own reviews, and request translation of review text. Mixly may process the published mix ID, reaction type, rating, review/comment text, review language, generated user ID, saved display name or nickname, timestamps, and related request metadata. Review or comment text and the display name or nickname at the time of submission may be visible to other users if shown in the community area.

If you request translation of community review/comment text, Mixly may send the text and target language to third-party translation service providers. Translation providers may process that text and related technical metadata under their own terms and privacy practices.

Messaging and notification service providers may process message content and related metadata under their own terms and privacy practices. Please do not include sensitive personal information in support, feedback, or suggestion messages unless it is necessary for your request.

6. Analytics

In app versions with Amplitude enabled, Mixly automatically sends usage and diagnostic events to Amplitude to understand how the app is used, improve it, and evaluate Premium features.

Analytics events may include a persistent, pseudonymous Amplitude device ID, session ID, event name, event time, platform, operating system name and version, app version, build number, app slug, app language, premium status, active tab, and device locale information such as device language, language tag, region, currency, measurement system, text direction, time zone, and 24-hour-clock setting. If you sign in with Apple or Google, Mixly also sends the pseudonymous Supabase account ID to link analytics across your signed-in devices.

Analytics event properties may include selected tab, previous tab, screen name, selected onboarding counts, selected strength identifiers, mix ID, mix name, ingredient count, strength level, favorite state, swipe direction, paywall source, package identifier, product identifier, package type, price, currency, restore status, purchase event status, share status, review rating, review text length, support and feedback message length, reaction ID, and similar product interaction metadata.

For Favorites search analytics, Mixly sends whether you entered a search query, the number of characters you entered, and the number of results. Mixly does not send the search text to Amplitude.

Performance and diagnostic events may include app startup time, Apple sign-in attempt duration, the reason, stage or error code for a failed sign-in, and the category of a cloud sync or restore failure.

Amplitude uses the analytics request IP address to derive an approximate location such as country, region or city when its GeoIP settings allow. Mixly uses this information for product analytics.

Successful premium purchase events may include Amplitude revenue fields when RevenueCat exposes product price data. Mixly does not use Amplitude for advertising, ad personalization, or cross-app tracking. We do not intentionally send your name, email address, precise GPS location, contacts, photos, support message text, or full community review/comment text to Amplitude.

7. Subscriptions and purchases

Mixly may offer premium features through in-app purchases, subscriptions, trials, or lifetime access where available. Purchase processing is handled by Apple App Store, Google Play, and RevenueCat. Mixly does not directly collect or store your full payment card details.

RevenueCat and the applicable app store may process information needed to show offerings, process purchases, validate receipts, manage subscriptions, restore purchases, and determine whether premium entitlements are active. This may include app user or device identifiers, product and package identifiers, purchase status, receipt or transaction data, entitlement status, store country, currency, price, timestamps, diagnostics, and related technical data.

RevenueCat purchase webhook events may be routed through Mixly backend services and messaging or notification service providers for operational purchase notifications. These webhook events may include product ID, store, price, currency, entitlement IDs, app user ID, transaction IDs, offering ID, purchase time, expiration time, and event time.

8. Sharing and links

Mixly lets you share mixes or the app through the operating system share sheet. When you choose to share a mix, Mixly may generate share text, a Mixly deep link, a smart share URL, and, where enabled, a temporary share-card image based on the mix name, ingredients, percentages, description, and app link. The destination app or person you choose to share with is controlled by you and is not controlled by Mixly.

Smart share-link pages and deep links may process the shared mix ID, request URL, user-agent, IP address, timestamps, and related technical request metadata so the link can open Mixly or redirect to the appropriate app store. Mixly also tracks basic share events in analytics, such as mix ID, source, share mode, and success or failure state.

When you share a Labs mix with a short link, Mixly stores a separate snapshot of its name, ingredient labels, percentages, and ingredient types in Supabase, together with a random link code, creation time, and your authenticated or anonymous session ID. Anyone with the link can read the recipe; your session ID and creation time are not returned. Editing or deleting the original mix does not change the snapshot. Shared snapshots are retained so sent links keep working; deleting your account removes its association with the snapshot and does not revoke the link.

Mix Shot lets you take a photo or choose an image from your photo library and add a mix recipe to it. Mixly requests camera or photo-library access when you choose the corresponding action, and permission to save an image when you save a Mix Shot. The original image, composed image, and draft references are processed and stored locally on your device; Mixly does not upload these photos or include them in account cloud snapshots or analytics. You can save the composed image to your photo library or share it through the operating system share sheet. Any app or person you choose to share with handles the image under its own policies. You can manage these permissions in your device settings.

9. Data we do not collect directly

In the current release, Mixly does not provide or use:

  • email-and-password registration or sign-in;
  • direct card payment processing by Mixly (subscription billing and purchase management are handled by App Store or Google Play);
  • advertising SDKs or ad personalization;
  • public social profiles, private chats, or direct messaging between users;
  • contacts, precise location, coarse location, microphone, or push notification permissions.

10. How we use information

We use information described in this policy to:

  • authenticate your account and sync or restore saved account data;
  • provide the app's core catalog, filtering, recommendation, favorites, and onboarding functionality;
  • load and update catalog and community content;
  • receive, route, review, and respond where possible to support, feedback, and content suggestion messages;
  • moderate, improve, approve, reject, and publish community mix suggestions;
  • operate community reactions, ratings, reviews, review editing, review deletion, and review translation features;
  • provide premium features, show subscription offerings, validate purchases, restore purchases, and manage premium entitlements;
  • generate share text, share cards, deep links, smart share links, and app store fallback links when you choose to share content;
  • measure app usage, feature performance, onboarding, favorites, community interactions, sharing, paywall flows, and purchase funnel events through analytics;
  • maintain security, prevent abuse, debug errors, and operate backend services;
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use and License Agreement.

11. Sharing information

We do not sell personal data. We do not share personal data for advertising tracking or ad personalization.

We may share or allow processing of information with service providers that help operate Mixly, including Supabase, Apple and Google for sign-in, Telegram and other messaging and notification service providers, Amplitude, RevenueCat, Apple App Store, Google Play, translation service providers, hosting providers, email providers, and technical infrastructure providers. These providers process information for service operation, hosting, analytics, subscription management, translation, security, support, and similar purposes.

We may also disclose information if required by law, legal process, government request, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of Mixly, users, or others.

12. Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it, clear app data, reset the app, or uninstall Mixly. If you sign in with Apple or Google, you may delete your account in the app; this deletes the authenticated account and its private cloud app-data snapshot. Signing out removes the local account data from that device after it has had an opportunity to sync.

Support, feedback, user profile records, analytics records, subscription entitlement records, smart share-link technical logs, community suggestion records, community reaction records, and community review records are retained only as long as reasonably needed for support, app improvement, catalog improvement, moderation, analytics, subscription management, sharing functionality, security, legal, and operational purposes.

You may delete your own community reviews in the app where that feature is available. You may request deletion of support correspondence, user profile records, community suggestion records, or community review records, or ask privacy questions, by contacting the public account-deletion request page or support@get-mixly.app. We may need to retain some information if required for legal, security, fraud prevention, billing, dispute-resolution, or legitimate operational reasons.

13. Children and minors

Mixly is not directed to children or minors. You must be at least 18 years old to use Mixly, or older if a higher age is required by the laws that apply to you. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or minors.

14. International processing

Mixly is operated from Georgia and uses service providers that may process information in other countries. These countries may have data protection laws that differ from the laws in your location.

15. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information handled by Mixly. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Mixly changes or when legal requirements change. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date and publish the revised policy through the app or the relevant public legal page.

17. Contact

For privacy, support, or legal questions, contact: support@get-mixly.app.
