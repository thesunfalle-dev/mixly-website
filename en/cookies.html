Cookie & Analytics Policy

Effective date: July 22, 2026

This page explains the website storage and optional analytics controls available on the Mixly website.

1. Your choice

Mixly asks before it enables optional analytics. You can accept or reject it without losing access to the website. To change your choice later, open analytics settings.

2. Strictly necessary browser storage

The website remembers your language, your analytics choice, and a one-time page-transition preference in browser storage. These values are not used for advertising, cross-site tracking, or profiling.

3. Optional analytics

If you allow analytics, we use a service provider to understand page visits, language changes, and clicks on app-store links. We do not record sessions, use heatmaps, or automatically capture interactions. More details are in our Privacy Policy.

4. More information

For the wider information-handling practices of Mixly, read our Privacy Policy. For questions, contact support@get-mixly.app.
