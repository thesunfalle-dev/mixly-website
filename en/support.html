Mixly Support

Last updated: April 25, 2026

Mixly is an adults-only informational catalog and recommendation reference for hookah/shisha flavor mixes. Mixly does not sell, ship, facilitate the sale of, or promote the purchase or consumption of tobacco, nicotine, alcohol, or controlled products.

Contact us

If you need help with Mixly, want to report an issue, suggest a catalog update, or ask a privacy or legal question, contact us by email:

support@get-mixly.app

What we can help with

  • app bugs, crashes, or loading issues;
  • catalog corrections, missing brands, flavors, or mix ideas;
  • feedback about recommendations, filters, favorites, and app usability;
  • privacy, terms, license, or other legal questions;
  • purchase or subscription questions if premium features are available in your app version.

Account deletion request

You can delete an Apple- or Google-connected Mixly account inside the app: Profile → Profile details → Delete profile and data. This removes the authenticated Mixly account and its private cloud state.

If you cannot access the app or account, email support@get-mixly.app with the subject “Mixly account deletion request”. Include the email address associated with Apple or Google, if one was shared with Mixly, and do not include passwords, payment-card details, or one-time codes. We may request information needed to verify the request and may retain limited data where required for billing, fraud prevention, security, or law.
