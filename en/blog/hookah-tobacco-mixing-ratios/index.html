Hookah Tobacco Mixing Ratios

Simple starting points for two and three flavors without unnecessary precision.

Ratios help you decide which flavor is the base, which one supports it and which one stays as an accent. They are a guide to a clear result, not a demand for laboratory precision.

When to use a 50/50 mix

Use equal parts when both flavors should be equally noticeable: mango and passion fruit, watermelon and melon, or strawberry and banana. If one is stronger, try 60/40 or 70/30.

70/30: let one flavor lead

With 70/30, the base takes most of the bowl and the second flavor adds character. It is a useful start for mango and passion fruit, peach and tea, or pineapple and coconut.

Use strong flavors sparingly

Mint, cooling, cinnamon, spices and bright citrus often work at 10–20%. Start at 80/20 or even 90/10: it is easier to add more next time than to rescue an overloaded bowl.

How to mix three flavors

Try 60/30/10 for a first three-flavor mix: base, supporting flavor and accent. Mango, passion fruit and mint is a clear example because every ingredient has a separate job.

Adjust after each bowl

Brand, cut, moisture, packing and heat all affect intensity. Change one share at a time. For flavor ideas, read our guide to hookah flavor combinations and save the good ones in Mixly.
